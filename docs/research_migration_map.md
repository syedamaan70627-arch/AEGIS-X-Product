# AEGIS-X Research Migration Map

This document establishes the formal mapping between the scientific source-of-truth Google Colab notebook (`research_source/AEGIS_X_01_Baseline.ipynb`) and the target production architecture (`aegis/`). It defines the functional breakdown of Modules 1 through 13, their inputs, outputs, core algorithms, fitted state, dependencies, production destinations, migration status, and scientific limitations.

---

## Scientific Principles & Limitations Preserved

As mandated by Phase B governance, the following empirical findings and scientific conclusions from the research notebook are preserved as ground truth:

1. **Module 6 Controlled Stress Failure**: Initial naive linear fusion failed under high synthetic stress (negative result). Module 6R resolved this via group-aware splits and stress-robust meta-fusion.
2. **Simple Fusion Collapse**: Simple/unweighted linear fusion collapsed toward uncertainty when exposed to multi-signal noise.
3. **OOD & Drift Redundancy**: Out-of-Distribution (OOD) distance and Concept Drift metrics showed partial functional redundancy on spatial shifts.
4. **Uncertainty Sensitivity**: Uncertainty estimation was identified in ablation experiments as the single most performance-sensitive component.
5. **Drift Removal Phenomenon**: Completely removing the concept drift signal unexpectedly improved AUPR in one specific benchmark evaluation scenario.
6. **Module 13 Statistical Inconclusiveness**: Statistical superiority of unified risk fusion over the single best individual signal was statistically inconclusive in Module 13 multi-seed bootstrap tests.
7. **Early Warning Reliability**: Temporal early warning alerts are promising for gradual degradation but are not universally reliable under abrupt/hard fault injections.
8. **Associative Failure Signatures**: Failure memory signatures are empirical associative signal pattern clusters, not confirmed causal root causes.
